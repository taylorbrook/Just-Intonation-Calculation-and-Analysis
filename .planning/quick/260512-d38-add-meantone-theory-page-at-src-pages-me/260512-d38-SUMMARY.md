## Discipline honored

- **Three-layer split.** Page imports only from `src/lib/`, `src/audio/`, `src/components/`. The new `centsToRatio` in `src/lib/cents.ts` is the project's boundary wrapping `xen-dev-utils.centsToValue`; the page imports `centsToRatio` (not raw `centsToValue`).
- **Pitfall #1 (cents is display-only).** The tempered fifths are irrational by construction and live entirely in the cents/Hz domain. NO `new Interval(...)` ever constructed from a cents-derived float. The cents arithmetic itself derives both halves of `1200·log₂(3/2) − (1/n)·1200·log₂(81/80)` from `pureFifth.cents` and `syntonic.cents` — projections of kernel-exact Intervals — so no ad-hoc `Math.log2` constants leak into the page.
- **R-01 (BigInt path).** Every kernel-exact anchor is constructed via `new Interval("...")` with a string ratio, exercising the BigInt Fraction path.
- **D-24 (Interval immutability).** Kernel anchors are constructed once at cell top and reused.
- **Pattern 4 / Pitfall #2 (cell-owned synth).** Synth lives in its own cell with no dependencies. `createSynth()` is lazy — the AudioContext is not allocated until the first user-click play. `invalidation.then(synth.dispose)` handles cell re-eval and cross-page navigation.
- **T-02-22 (XSS-safe DOM).** `variantsTable` constructs every cell via `createElement` + `textContent` — no `innerHTML` interpolation of dynamic values. Only `playInterval` returns its own button element, which is `createElement`-built upstream and appended directly.
