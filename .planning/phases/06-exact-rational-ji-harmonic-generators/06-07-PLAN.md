| T-06-18 | Tampering (laundered JI) | tempered ED output + Send-to | mitigate | ED renders via the tempered scaleTable (no ratio column, D-01) + badge (D-02); Send-to serializes cents-per-line (D-03), never ratios — no float-derived fraction is ever presented or persisted as exact JI (SURF-06). |
